Case Setup

Emily Harris must rank two growth proposals for New Heritage’s capital committee in 2010.

生产部门有两项增长提案；Harris需要为资本预算委员会排出优先级。

THE COMPANY

An established doll franchise.

New Heritage combines dolls, accessories, retail experiences and character licensing. Production is its most asset-intensive division; customer attachment to the brand is a shared asset.

THE CONSTRAINT
≈15% of EBITDA

The company’s historical capital budget, shared across production, retail and licensing. Funding and management capacity are scarce, so two attractive proposals may not both receive approval.

Which growth route should receive priority if both cannot be funded?

Methods & sources

Base: g 2%; Clothing r 8.4%, Custom r 9.0%; tax 40%; 365-day workbook convention; immediate loss relief; no extra IT staff charge. Original Excel checks retain their original g 3% inputs.

IRR includes terminal value in 2020. Payback excludes a terminal-value sale; both paybacks extend annual operating cash flows beyond 2020 using the chosen terminal-growth rule. Fractional years use interpolation.

IT: the 2011 staff-cost test adds up to $435K before tax. Include replacement spending or displaced contribution when resources have another valuable use. A company-wide cost transfer alone does not change cash flow.
